Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA6 Auto Clipper (the "App"), a locally run desktop video clipping and social publishing tool, handles data when you use it. It applies to the App's current desktop publishing implementation and this informational website.

1. What the App accesses

You can supply a video file or a public/otherwise accessible video or livestream URL. The App processes video and audio locally to identify highlights and generate clips, captions and hooks. If you choose to connect a social account, the App requests access needed to publish content on your behalf.

For YouTube, the App requests the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This grants permission to upload videos to the channel you authorize; it does not request your YouTube password. The App uses YouTube API Services. Please also review Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Data stored on your computer

Generated video clips, subtitles, hook text, and publishing status/receipts are saved in local App output folders. After YouTube authorization, the App writes an access/refresh credential file named youtube_token.json next to the App's Python files, and reads a locally provided OAuth client configuration (client_secrets.json). For optional Instagram or TikTok connections, account identifiers and access tokens may be stored in the local .env file. The App also keeps a local SQLite publishing queue to avoid repeated uploads and record outcomes.

Protect these files and do not publish them to a public repository or share them with others. They are not embedded in this website.

3. How data is used and shared

The App uses locally stored credentials only for the account connection and upload operations you enable. When publishing is enabled, your completed clip and its title, caption, hashtags and selected visibility settings are sent to the authorized platform through its API. Uploaded content is handled according to that platform's policies. The App may access YouTube to resolve a video or livestream URL for clipping, independent of the publishing connection.

The current App is designed to run on your PC; the developer does not operate a separate cloud service for receiving your video files or OAuth tokens. The App's current source does not intentionally sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to advertising networks. This informational website does not include advertising or analytics scripts; its web hosting provider may nonetheless process routine connection logs.

4. Consent and publishing controls

Account connection requires Google or the applicable platform's authorization flow. Auto-publishing is turned off by default and becomes active only after you explicitly enable it and confirm your rights to the content. You can select YouTube upload visibility (private, unlisted, or public) in the App. The TikTok integration requires individual review and consent for each clip. Stopping future publishing does not delete posts already made, and an upload already in progress may finish.

5. Revoking access and deleting local data

You can revoke GTA6 Auto Clipper's Google access in your Google Account connection settings (or at Google's third-party access settings). To stop future local uploads, first disable auto-publishing and close the App. To remove the locally stored YouTube credentials, delete youtube_token.json after revoking access. You can also remove locally generated clips and the local publishing queue in clipper_output if you no longer need them. Remove Instagram/TikTok tokens from .env if applicable.

Deleting local files or revoking authorization does not remove videos already published on YouTube or other platforms. Delete those from the respective platform account. If you need assistance with personal data or deletion, contact the support email below.

6. Retention and security

Local clips, publishing history and credential files remain on the computer until they are removed by the user or replaced by App operations. Google access tokens are refreshed when required by authorized uploads. Users are responsible for safeguarding their PC and keeping credential files private. No security mechanism can guarantee absolute protection.

7. Third-party services

Publishing uses YouTube API Services and, if enabled, Instagram and TikTok APIs. Third parties may collect and process data once you authorize or upload content; consult their privacy policies. The App may rely on third-party libraries for local video processing and transcription. These may need to download software or model files during installation.
