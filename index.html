Desktop video tool

Turn your authorized gameplay footage into standout shorts.

GTA6 Auto Clipper helps creators find exciting moments in their own or properly licensed gameplay videos and livestreams. It creates vertical highlights, adds synchronized subtitles and opening hooks, and can publish to connected social accounts.

Publishing remains under your control. YouTube and Instagram automatic publishing is off until you connect your account and enable it. TikTok publishing requires individual review and consent for each clip. Only upload footage you have rights to use.

How YouTube access works

When you connect your YouTube channel, the desktop app asks for the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It uses that permission to upload clips you choose to publish. The Google authorization token is stored on your computer, not in this website.

Uploads only occur after you authorize the account and explicitly turn publishing on. You can disable future publishing in the app or revoke access from your Google Account connections.
